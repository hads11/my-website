💜 Getting Started

Where to Begin: Your First Music Videos!

In my opinion, the best way to start is by throwing yourself into the deep end! Like I mentioned on the home page, the first MV I ever watched was "Blood, Sweat, & Tears", and I was hooked immediately. For your first few BTS music videos, I definitely recommend "Fire", "I Need U", "DNA", "Dope", and "Fake Love". Something BTS always brings the heat on is their visual aesthetic, and these music videos are practically art pieces fit for a museum.

⭐ KPOP Terms

ARMY Dictionary: Terms You Should Know!

In any KPOP fandom, there are a few familiar terms that the KPOP community use! "Annyeonghaseyo"- This is the formal way to say "hello." "Sasaeng" - This means "an obsessive, crazy fan," which is what we DON'T want to be. Finally, "Saranghaeyo" - This is the formal way to say "I love you." These are just a few terms to know that make it easier to know what's going on when you're watching fan compilations and live performances!

🗣️ Member Guide

Meeting the Members: Who's Who and Ages!

Memorizing the members is a little hard at first, but you'll get it in no time! Kim Namjoon, "RM", is the leader of the group. He was born in 1994 and is part of the rap line. Kim Seokjin, "Worldwide Handsome," is the oldest of the group, born in 1992, and is part of the vocal line. Min Yoongi, "Suga", is the second oldest, born in 1993 and part of the rap line. Jung Hoseok, "J-Hope", is the third oldest, born in 1994, the best dancer, and he's part of the rap line. Park Jimin, "ChimChim", is the third youngest, born in 1995, and is part of the vocal line. Kim Taehyung, "V", is the second youngest, born in 1995, is part of the vocal line. Lastly, Jeon Jungkook, "The Golden Maknae", is the youngest of the group, born in 1997, and is the last of the vocal line. It's a lot, I know, but give it a few weeks and it'll be hard to forget!

🎤 Bias & Bias Wrecker

Finding Your Bias and Your Bias Wrecker!

Lastly and arguably one of the most important parts of being an ARMY, we're going to find your bias and bias wrecker. Your bias is just your favorite member in the group, and your bias wrecker is essentially your second favorite, or who you feel could take your bias' place, thus 'bias wrecker.' For example, my bias has always been RM, but lately, my bias wrecker has been Jimin. If you're having trouble picking, there are plenty of online quizzes that'll pick for you! Just search up 'BTS bias quiz' and you'll get tons of results. I know, it's really hard to choose, and honestly it's easier to just be OT7.💜
